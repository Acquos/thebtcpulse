FAQ

What is the Bitcoin price today?
The BTC Pulse shows the live Bitcoin price (BTC/USDT) updated every 30 seconds, alongside the change versus the current weekly entry price.
How does the weekly Bitcoin signal work?
Every Thursday we publish a directional signal — bullish or bearish — derived from a proprietary macro liquidity model built on public economic data.
What is the historical accuracy of the signal?
The BTC Pulse publishes its full weekly track record — entry price, exit price and result for every past week — plus the year-to-date direction accuracy.
Is The BTC Pulse free?
Yes. The live price, the weekly signal, the 4 targets, the full track record and the chart are all free, with no signup required.
How accurate is the signal?
We publish the full track record: the yearly direction hit rate and the historical hit rate of each of the four targets, updated every week.
